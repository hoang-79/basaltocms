4. We publish a version only once it has passed both checks. There is no entitlement to publication; if we decline,

The Marketplace accepts free and paid listings. The following applies to paid listings:

2. **Price:** you set the price of a paid listing; basaltocms.com shows it in CHF, EUR and USD.

4. **Payout:** via Stripe Connect. You open a connected account with Stripe and accept its terms; Stripe verifies

5. **Seller:** you sell the licence yourself, in your own name and for your own account; we broker the sale,
