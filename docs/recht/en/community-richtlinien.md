# Community Guidelines

> **Draft — legal review pending.** Version 0.2 · As of 25.10.2026 · not yet in force.
> The German version prevails; translations are for information only.

The Basalto Community is the place for questions, answers, ideas and projects around Basalto. To keep it that way,
these rules apply — to everyone, including our team.

## 1. What they apply to

To the forum on basaltocms.com with profiles, posts, reactions, polls and private conversations, and to reviews and
replies in the Marketplace. They supplement the [Terms of Use](nutzungsbedingungen.md).

## 2. Basic rules

- **Respect:** Criticise things, not people. Stay polite, even if you disagree.
- **On topic:** One thread per question, in the appropriate forum, with a meaningful title. Search first to see
  whether the question has already been asked.
- **Helpful:** Describe your problem so that others can follow it — version, steps, error message — and mark the
  answer that helped.
- **Honest:** Anyone who speaks for a company or recommends their own product says so.
- **Discreet:** Do not publish credentials, keys or personal data — not even your own.

## 3. What is not permitted

- unlawful content, such as defamation, threats, incitement to violence or criminal offences;
- discrimination and incitement to hatred on the grounds of origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability
  or other characteristics (Art. 261bis SCC);
- harassment, intimidation and repeatedly pursuing a person;
- other people's personal data without their consent, in particular addresses, telephone numbers or pictures;
- pornographic content, content glorifying violence or content otherwise harmful to minors;
- malware, instructions for attacks on other people's systems and links to them;
- spam, off-topic advertising, chain letters and collecting contacts;
- content to which you hold no rights, such as copied texts, images or code without permission;
- deception: false identities, posing as the team, fake reviews or reactions, multiple accounts to circumvent votes
  or bans;
- automatically generated posts in large numbers.

## 4. Security vulnerabilities

Report security vulnerabilities in Basalto or on basaltocms.com **never publicly**, but confidentially to
email@basaltocms.com. We confirm receipt, fix the vulnerability and, on request, credit you in the release notes.

## 5. Profiles, signatures and images

Name, profile picture, cover image and signature must comply with these rules; no advertising, no third-party trade
marks as your own identity. Choose a name that does not mislead anyone.

## 6. Private conversations

Only the participants see conversations. We do not read along. If a participant reports a message, moderators see
that message in order to review the report. These rules also apply in conversations.

## 7. Moderation

We moderate so that the Community remains useful and safe. Depending on severity and repetition, we may:

- point out a problem, edit a post (marked as edited), move, close or hide it;
- review posts by new members before publication;
- move posts to the trash or delete them;
- issue a warning;
- ban your account from the Community temporarily or permanently and mark it as a spam account.

Automatic protective measures — a word filter, a pause between posts, the check in the browser — take effect without
a human decision, but do not ban anyone permanently.

If a measure affects you, we inform you with a statement of reasons: what we did, because of which content, based on
which rule or which law, whether the decision was automated and how you can challenge it (section 9). We record
every measure in the moderation log.

## 8. Reporting

If content breaches these rules or the law, use “Report” on the post, on the profile or in the conversation. Reviews
in the Marketplace can also be reported without an account. Reports of infringed rights are governed by the
instructions under [Copyright](urheberrecht.md). We review every report promptly, carefully and without
arbitrariness, and inform you of the outcome. If someone repeatedly sends manifestly unfounded reports, we may block
them from reporting for a period of time.

## 9. Objection

If you disagree with a measure or with the decision on your report, write to us within six months at
email@basaltocms.com, with the link or the statement of reasons you received. We review the decision again — by a person
who did not make it, where possible [[Platzhalter: Who reviews while Nguyen Development is a one-person business?]] —
and reverse it if it was not justified. Recourse to the courts remains open.

## 10. Responsibility

Authors are responsible for their posts. We provide the platform, do not check posts before publication — except
those of new members — and act as soon as we learn of unlawful content. Answers in the Community are not commitments
by us, even if they come from the team; only the Licence Agreement and the GTC are binding.

## 11. Changes

We adapt these guidelines when the Community grows or the law changes. We announce material changes in the
Community. The version in force is shown at the top.
