## 8. Reporting infringements

If content on basaltocms.com infringes your rights — a post, an image, a listing in the Marketplace — report it using
the “Report” button next to the content or by e-mail to email@basaltocms.com. So that we can review it, the report
needs:

1. the exact address (URL) of the content;
2. which right is infringed and why, for copyright with a reference to the original;
3. your name and e-mail address — except for reports concerning depictions of child sexual abuse;
4. a statement that your information is correct and complete to the best of your knowledge.

We confirm receipt, review carefully and promptly, and remove or block the content if it infringes rights. We inform
the person who published it, stating reasons; they may respond (section 9 of the
[Community Guidelines](community-richtlinien.md)). We may leave manifestly unfounded or abusive reports unprocessed.

Anyone who infringes our rights must expect claims for injunctive relief, removal and damages (Art. 62 CopA, Art. 9
UCA). Swiss law applies; place of jurisdiction is Winterthur unless mandatory provisions stipulate otherwise.
