# Withdrawal and Refunds

> **Draft — legal review pending.** Version 0.2 · As of 25.10.2026 · not yet in force.
> The German version prevails; translations are for information only.

This page explains when you can cancel a purchase or a subscription and how you get your money back. These rules
supplement the [GTC](agb.md); in the event of contradictions, the text more favourable to you prevails.

## 1. Overview

| What | Return | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Licence (Site or Agency) and renewal | within 14 days without giving reasons — for everyone | section 2 |
| SaaS — trial | free of charge; nothing starts automatically afterwards | section 4 |
| SaaS — subscription | can be cancelled at any time to the end of the month; consumers in the EU: withdrawal within 14 days | sections 3 and 4 |
| Purchase in the Marketplace | within 14 days without giving reasons — for everyone | section 5 |

## 2. Returning a licence

You can return a purchased licence within 14 days of purchase without giving reasons — as a business or as a
private individual, from Switzerland or from abroad. For the renewal of a licence, the period runs from the renewal
date. The return right does not lapse if you have already received or used the key.

After the return, we refund the full amount (section 6), and the key is placed on the revocation list: after the
next update it is no longer valid, and the software keeps running as it would without a key. Sub-licences of an
Agency licence end with it.

## 3. Statutory right of withdrawal in the EU

This section applies to consumers resident in the European Union who conclude a contract via basaltocms.com.

### Withdrawal information

**Right of withdrawal.** You have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The
withdrawal period will expire after 14 days from the day of the conclusion of the contract. To exercise the right of
withdrawal, you must inform us (Basalto — a product of nguyen-development.ch, Hoang Nguyen, Hohfurristrasse 24, 8408
Winterthur, Switzerland, e-mail email@basaltocms.com) of your decision to withdraw from this contract by an unequivocal
statement (e.g. an e-mail or a message from the customer account). You may use the model withdrawal form in section 7,
but it is not obligatory. To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient for you to send your communication
concerning your exercise of the right of withdrawal before the withdrawal period has expired.

**Effects of withdrawal.** If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse to you all payments received from
you without undue delay and in any event not later than 14 days from the day on which we are informed about your
decision to withdraw from this contract. We will carry out such reimbursement using the same means of payment as you
used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly agreed otherwise; in any event, you will not incur any
fees as a result of such reimbursement. If you requested to begin the performance of services — such as a SaaS
subscription — during the withdrawal period, you shall pay us an amount which is in proportion to what has been
provided until you have communicated us your withdrawal from this contract, in comparison with the full coverage of
the contract.

### Early expiry

For digital content that is not supplied on a tangible medium, the statutory right of withdrawal lapses if we have
begun performance of the contract after you have expressly consented to our beginning before the end of the
withdrawal period and have acknowledged that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal. **We do not make use of
this** — the return under section 2 and section 5 always applies to licences and purchases in the Marketplace.

## 4. SaaS: trial, subscription and cancellation

- **Trial:** 14 days free of charge and without a payment method. Nothing starts automatically afterwards; unless
  you choose a plan, neither a subscription nor an invoice arises.
- **Subscription:** monthly from the day you choose a plan; payment is made in advance. If you choose the plan
  during the trial, payment begins only when the trial ends.
- **Cancellation:** at any time under Administration › Account › Export and cancellation, to the end of the paid
  month. Until then, you can undo the cancellation. After that, the account is locked for 30 days and then deleted;
  you can create the export of your sites and data at any time before then.
- **Refund:** we do not refund months already begun, except after a withdrawal under section 3, if we have charged
  twice or if we end the subscription prematurely through no fault of yours — in that case on a pro rata basis.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the end of the month, **upgrading** immediately — with a card, we charge the
  difference pro rata; with a QR-bill, the new price applies from the next invoice.

## 5. Purchases in the Marketplace

Paid themes and plugins come from authors; we broker the sale ([Author Terms](autoren-bedingungen.md), section 7). The
seller is the author; we accept the payment and handle the return. You can return a paid listing, like a licence, within
14 days of purchase without giving reasons, even if you have already downloaded it. Your entitlement to updates of the
listing then ends.

If a listing is defective or does not match its description, your statutory rights also apply; let us know and we
will mediate with the author.

## 6. Requesting a refund

Write from the customer account or to email@basaltocms.com stating:

1. the e-mail address of your customer account;
2. the invoice number or the order;
3. for payment by QR-bill: your IBAN and the name of the account holder.

We confirm receipt, refund within 14 days by the same means you used to pay and issue a credit note. For card
payments, depending on the bank, it may take a few days longer for the amount to appear on your statement.

## 8. Switzerland and other countries

Swiss law does not provide for a general right of withdrawal for purchases on the internet. You are covered by the
return under section 2 and the rules on the subscription under section 4. Mandatory rights under the law of your
place of residence remain reserved.
